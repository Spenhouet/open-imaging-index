This entry covers the structural MRI cohort of a 2017 Neuropsychologia report by Kim, Mattek, Burr and Whalen. In
earlier fMRI studies from the Whalen lab at Dartmouth College, about one in five participants noticed the fearful or
happy faces hidden by backward masking and were dropped from the functional analyses. Here the authors pooled the
anatomical scans of two such studies and compared grey matter volume between participants who noticed the hidden
images and those who did not. The aware group had larger posterior thalamus volume, including both pulvinar nuclei.
The T1-weighted scans are not released; per-subject voxel-based morphometry maps are public on NeuroVault.

## Composition

64 young adults took part, 39 of them women, with a mean age of 19.6 years. 37 came from a study that masked full
faces and 27 from a study that masked eye whites. Everyone was screened for psychiatric illness and none had taken
psychotropic medication. A post-scan interview classed 12 participants as aware of the masked targets and 52 as
unaware. State and trait anxiety scores were also collected.

## Acquisition

All scans were acquired at the Dartmouth Brain Imaging Center on a 3T Philips Intera Achieva with an 8-channel head
coil. The anatomical sequence was a sagittal MPRAGE with 160 slices at roughly 1 mm resolution. Both source studies
also ran block-design task fMRI with 17 ms targets followed by masks, but the functional data are not part of this
report or its deposit.

## Annotations

The NeuroVault collection has one smoothed, Jacobian-modulated grey matter map per subject, produced with VBM8 and
DARTEL in SPM8 and resampled to 1.5 mm MNI space, plus the group t map for aware against unaware. Each subject map
carries age, sex, intracranial volume, source study and awareness group as metadata.

## Known limitations

- Only derived grey matter maps are public; the T1-weighted images are not shared and have no published terms.
- The aware group is small (12 subjects), and the sample is narrow in age.
- The study code in the NeuroVault metadata is numeric and its mapping to the two source studies is not stated.
