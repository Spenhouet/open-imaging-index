SpineWeb Dataset 7 is a small MRI collection for locating and segmenting the intervertebral discs of the lower spine. It
was contributed to SpineWeb, a collection of spine imaging datasets, by Guoyan Zheng of the Institute for Surgical
Technology and Biomechanics at the University of Bern. It is described only by a short SpineWeb entry, which cites no
paper. The same group published a disc localization and segmentation method in IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging in
2015 (Chen et al.), validated on 3D T2-weighted turbo spin echo images of 35 patients from two studies; neither the
paper abstract nor the SpineWeb entry says whether the 15 released patients are drawn from that cohort.

## Composition

15 anonymized patients, one 3D T2-weighted turbo spin echo volume each. Every volume shows at least 7 intervertebral
discs, covering the levels from T11 to L5. Images and masks are NIfTI files, with one mask volume per patient. The page
gives no age, sex or diagnosis information.

## Acquisition

All patients were scanned on a 1.5 T Siemens Magnetom Sonata. Sequence parameters, voxel size and scan sites are not
stated on the page.

## Annotations

Each disc has a manual reference segmentation, delivered as one binary mask volume per patient. The page does not say
who drew the masks or how they were checked.

## Known limitations

- Very small: 15 patients from one scanner model and one sequence.
- No demographics, clinical data, acquisition parameters or annotation protocol are published.
- Access is by contacting the contributor; no license or data use terms are published for this dataset.
- The SpineWeb host (spineweb.digitalimaginggroup.ca) did not resolve when this entry was checked on 2026-10-09; the
  description was read from an Internet Archive copy of the page from June 2024.
