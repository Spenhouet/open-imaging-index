This Zenodo record shares minimally preprocessed resting-state fMRI from people in China with obsessive-compulsive
disorder (OCD) and healthy controls. It was released with a study that models OCD as a mix of latent disease factors
and relates each person's factor expression to symptom dimensions and to the outcome of ventral capsulotomy, a
neurosurgical lesion procedure for treatment-refractory OCD. The record is under CC BY 4.0 and needs no account.

## Composition

The baseline set holds 104 patients with OCD and 82 healthy controls, drawn anonymously from the authors'
institutional database. The phenotype table lists two collection sites, the Institute of Neuroscience (142 people)
and RuiJin Hospital (44). It also flags 15 people (12 patients, 3 controls) as filtered out of the authors' analysis.
Of the baseline patients, 27 refractory cases had a ventral capsulotomy and complete MRI and clinical follow-up; they
come as two separate archives with scans before and after surgery, plus a table of the interval between surgery and
follow-up. Clinical scores are the Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale (Y-BOCS), the Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale
and the 17-item Hamilton Depression Rating Scale, together with age, sex, education, handedness, illness duration
and age at onset.

## Acquisition

Only resting-state functional images are shared, one NIfTI file per person in the baseline archive. They were
minimally preprocessed with Statistical Parametric Mapping (SPM); the record points to two earlier papers by the
same group for the pipeline, the inclusion criteria and the surgical procedure. Scanner, field strength and sequence
parameters are not stated in the record.

## Known limitations

- No raw or structural images are included, so the data cannot be reprocessed from scratch.
- Part of this cohort was used in earlier publications, so findings are not independent of that work.
- The two sites are not separated in the archive, and acquisition details per site are not documented.
- Some clinical values are missing and marked with a slash in the tables.
- The capsulotomy archives are RAR files and their contents were not checked for this entry.
