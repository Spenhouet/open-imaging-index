BREAST-DIAGNOSIS is a collection of diagnostic breast imaging from 88 women, contributed by B. Nicolas Bloch, Ashali
Jain and C. Carl Jaffe and hosted by The Cancer Imaging Archive. The cases cover high-risk patients without cancer,
ductal carcinoma in situ, fibroadenomas and invasive lobular and ductal carcinomas. Images were released in November
2011 and received a DOI in 2015. A clinical spreadsheet adds BI-RADS findings from the MRI report and abstracts of the
pathology report.

## Composition

The collection holds 148 studies, 429 series and 105,050 DICOM images (60.87 GB). Breast MRI covers 84 subjects in
112 studies; 24 of them have more than one MRI time point. Digital mammograms or radiographs of surgical specimens
exist for 17 subjects, and 3 subjects have whole-body PET/CT studies. The
spreadsheet lists 51 cases, 50 of which have images, and gives the affected side, a free-text background, the
pathology diagnosis, ER, PR, HER2, Ki67 and E-cadherin results, Oncotype DX scores where available, the age decade,
the BI-RADS category and the report impression.

## Acquisition

MRI was acquired on Philips Achieva and Intera scanners at 1.5 T. Most MRI studies contain a T2-weighted turbo spin
echo series, a STIR series and BLISS dynamic contrast-enhanced series; a few add T1-weighted, diffusion tensor or ADC
series. Magnevist was injected over 6 to 7 seconds at 3 ml/s, with a dose in millilitres equal to 10% of body weight in
pounds, and the first dynamic series started one minute after injection began. Mammography came from Lorad (Hologic)
units and PET/CT from a GE Discovery STE. Study dates range from 2006 to 2009.

## Annotations

The spreadsheet notes the BI-RADS category and pathology diagnosis per case. There are no segmentations.

## Known limitations

- Clinical and pathology entries are free text with inconsistent wording and cover only part of the cohort.
- The age decade is given for 19 of the imaged subjects; no other demographics are provided.
- The DICOM patient weight tag is in kilograms, while the contrast dose rule uses pounds.
- One MRI field strength; mammography and PET/CT exist for few subjects.
