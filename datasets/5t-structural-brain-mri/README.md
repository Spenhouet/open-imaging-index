This collection holds high-resolution structural MRI of the brain, acquired on a 5 Tesla system. It was deposited on Science Data Bank in August 2026 by Xinmao Ma and Rui Wang of West China Second University Hospital, Sichuan University. No paper or data descriptor is linked to the record, so everything known about it comes from the short record description and its keywords.

## Composition

According to the record, about 100 people took part, with ages from 6 years up to adulthood. The exact count, the upper age limit, the sex split and any clinical characteristics of the participants are not stated. Besides the structural scans, the depositors include additional derived images. Version 1 is a single file of about 1.8 GB.

## Acquisition

All images come from a 5 T MRI scanner. The scanner model, vendor and sequence parameters are not given on the record page; the description says they are documented in metadata that ships with the data. MP2RAGE is one of the record's keywords, which suggests this sequence was used, but the page does not confirm which images are included.

## Known limitations

Public information is limited to the record description, so cohort composition, inclusion criteria and acquisition details cannot be checked before access. Files are only released after the depositor approves a request that names the applicant, their institution and their research purpose. Use is restricted to scientific research, re-identification is forbidden, and the data may not be passed on without permission.
