The Sniffing out Covid project at Wageningen University & Research, funded by ZonMw, followed people whose sense of
smell stayed impaired after a SARS-CoV-2 infection. It has three research lines: the COVORTS cohort, followed for a
year with questionnaires and home smell and taste tests; long COVORTS, a questionnaire cohort of people with smell
problems lasting six months or more; and an (f)MRI sub-study. This entry covers the (f)MRI sub-study. Its scans are
not released. The shared DANS record holds tabular data from the COVORTS cohort under a data transfer agreement, and
states that derived imaging values will follow once the project ends.

## Composition

The (f)MRI preprint analysed 48 patients aged 19 to 60 (38 women, 10 men, mean age 47.4). 21 had quantitative smell
loss by Sniffin' Sticks score (8 anosmic, 13 hyposmic) and 27 reported parosmia. 29 came from the longitudinal
COVORTS cohort and 19 joined only for imaging. Infection had been confirmed by PCR or antigen self-test, on average
472 days before inclusion. Patients were scanned between January 2022 and June 2024. The preprint compares them with
49 normosmic controls from a separate Wageningen study, which this record does not include. The data record had
planned 50 patients in two groups of 25.

## Acquisition

All scans were made on a 3 T Philips Elition X with a 32-channel head coil. Each session had a coronal T2-weighted
scan of the olfactory bulbs, a 3D T1-weighted TFE whole-brain scan at 0.8 mm, and multiband gradient-echo EPI fMRI at
2.2 mm during an odour task with chocolate and beef odours, blanks and matching food pictures. The data record
planned a follow-up scan for patients first scanned within six months of infection; the preprint does not describe
follow-up scans.

## Annotations

Two trained raters manually segmented the olfactory bulbs in ITK-SNAP and reconciled differences above 10%. In 2
patients no bulbs were visible. Grey matter was segmented with CAT12 in SPM12.

## Known limitations

- The images are not shared; only derived volumes and scores are announced for release.
- Smell tests and the parosmia questionnaire were not done on the scan day.
- The patient group is mostly female and older than the controls in the preprint.
- Results come from a preprint that was not yet peer reviewed when this entry was written.
