This entry covers the task fMRI cohort behind a 2025 PLOS One study from Iran University of Medical Sciences and the
University of Tehran. The authors scanned adults with major depressive disorder (MDD) who had attempted suicide one to
four weeks earlier, depressed patients with no history of suicidal behavior, and healthy controls while they solved
Raven progressive matrices, and compared regional activation between the groups. The scans themselves are not public.
A CC0 deposit on Harvard Dataverse holds a clinical table and amplitude of low-frequency fluctuation (ALFF) maps,
packed as one archive per group.

## Composition

Sixty adults were analysed, 20 per group: suicide attempters with MDD, MDD without an attempt, and controls. Sixty-two
were recruited; two were dropped only to make the groups equal in size. Groups were matched on age, sex and education,
and 50 of the 60 participants were women. Mean age was about 34 years in every group. Attempters came from the
poisoning ward of Baharloo Hospital in Tehran and were scanned roughly a week after discharge.

## Acquisition

All scanning took place on a 3T Siemens system with a 64-channel head coil at the Iranian National Brain Mapping Lab.
Each participant had a T1-weighted MPRAGE (1 mm slices, 176 slices) and an echo-planar BOLD run of 220 volumes (TR
3 s, TE 30 ms, 44 axial slices) during 24 Raven trials, with EEG recorded at the same time. Preprocessing used SPM12;
group statistics used DPABI.

## Annotations

There are no image labels. Participants completed questionnaires on depression, hopelessness, suicidal ideation,
reasons for living, impulsivity, aggression and anxiety, and task accuracy and reaction times were recorded.

## Known limitations

- Raw and preprocessed MRI and the EEG are not shared; only derived ALFF maps and a clinical table are public.
- The derived maps are in standard space and cannot be reprocessed with another pipeline.
- Small single-site sample, mostly women. Table 1 lists age ranges down to 16 years, although the text gives 18 to 55
  as the inclusion range.
