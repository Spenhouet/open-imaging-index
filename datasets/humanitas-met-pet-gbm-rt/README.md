This single-centre cohort comes from a phase II trial at IRCCS Humanitas Research Hospital near Milan, Italy, in which
adults with newly diagnosed glioblastoma had maximal safe surgery followed by hypofractionated radiotherapy with
concurrent and adjuvant temozolomide. Before radiotherapy, each patient had a planning CT, brain MRI and
[11C]methionine PET/CT. The analysis asked whether methionine uptake reveals residual tumour that MRI misses, whether it
changes the radiotherapy target volumes, and whether uptake measures predict survival. The authors deposited the
underlying data as a restricted Zenodo record.

## Composition

Of 97 patients enrolled between August 2013 and April 2016, 93 had both MRI and methionine PET and form the cohort:
33 women and 60 men, median age 61 years (range 23 to 77). All tumours were IDH wild type; 53 were MGMT methylated and
40 unmethylated. Inclusion required age 18 to 70 and a Karnofsky score of at least 70. Extent of resection, overall and
progression-free survival over a median follow-up of 72 months, and neuropsychological scores are reported.

## Acquisition

Within one month of surgery, patients had a planning CT, FLAIR and post-contrast T1-weighted MPRAGE MRI, and PET after
300 to 500 MBq of [11C]methionine, started 10 to 15 minutes after injection and corrected for attenuation with CT. The
images were co-registered. Scanner models and MRI field strength are not given.

## Annotations

Contrast-enhancing residual tumour was measured on postoperative MRI. A biological tumour volume was defined on PET
with a tumour-to-background threshold of 1.5, and SUVmax and TBRmax were recorded. Two clinical target volumes were
outlined for radiotherapy, one around the cavity and residual tumour and one covering the FLAIR abnormality, and
changes driven by PET uptake were noted.

## Known limitations

- Not openly available. The Zenodo record lists no files, and access is granted only on request; whether the
  deposit holds images or only study tables is not stated.
- Single centre, with PET acquired after surgery rather than before.
- No scanner or field strength details are reported.
