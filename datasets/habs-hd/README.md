The Health and Aging Brain Study: Health Disparities (HABS-HD) is a community-based longitudinal cohort run by the
Institute for Translational Research at the University of North Texas Health Science Center in Fort Worth, Texas. It
studies brain ageing and Alzheimer's disease across non-Hispanic Black, Hispanic (mostly Mexican American) and
non-Hispanic White adults, and grew out of the earlier HABLE study. Data are released to approved researchers about
every six months, with raw images held at LONI.

## Composition

The methods paper describes data release 6: 3,840 partners (1,066 non-Hispanic Black, 1,425 Hispanic, 1,349
non-Hispanic White), of whom 2,379 are women. At baseline 2,788 were cognitively unimpaired, 804 had mild cognitive
impairment and 236 had dementia. Enrolment is open from age 30, and follow-up visits are every 24 months. Besides
imaging, the study collects clinical labs, plasma biomarkers (amyloid, tau, NfL, GFAP), APOE genotypes, genome-wide SNP
data, whole genome sequencing, neuropsychological tests and area deprivation scores.

## Acquisition

MRI was acquired on a Siemens 3 T Skyra (March 2017 to December 2020) and then a Siemens 3 T Magnetom Vida. The
protocol includes MPRAGE, FLAIR, a high-resolution T2w hippocampal scan, DTI, a gradient recalled echo (GRE) sequence,
arterial spin labeling and resting-state BOLD; from August 2024 a T2w SPACE scan and a multi-echo GRE were added and
ASL changed from pulsed to pseudo-continuous. The paper notes that the GRE data can be used for SWI and quantitative
susceptibility maps. Amyloid PET uses florbetaben and tau PET uses PI-2620, mostly on Siemens Biograph Vision 450
PET/CT scanners.

## Annotations

No manual image labels. Released derived data include FreeSurfer regional thickness, hippocampal and subfield volumes,
white matter hyperintensity volumes, DTI tract measures, and amyloid and tau PET clinical reads and SUVRs.

## Known limitations

- The Hispanic group is about 92% Mexican American, which limits generalisation to other Hispanic groups.
- Scanner and protocol changed twice, so harmonisation is needed for pooled analyses.
- Not every partner has every scan; for example only part of the cohort has a tau PET read.
- Per-sequence image counts, including GRE/SWI counts, are not published by the study.
