This dataset holds the brain MRI from a social neuroscience study at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) on how a shared national culture changes the way people perceive faces of their own and other ethnic groups. Participants saw faces of Chinese, Indian, Arab and Caucasian women, each preceded by a photo of a landmark that pointed either to an ethnic homeland (for example the Taj Mahal) or to Singapore itself (for example the Merlion). The authors report that same- and other-race faces engaged similar brain networks only after the Singapore context, which they call an enlarged in-group effect. The images were deposited in DR-NTU (Data) by Gianluca Esposito and published in February 2022 under CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Composition

The record contains 54 zip archives named CUET_0001 to CUET_0056 (numbers 0039 and 0040 are missing), one per participant, plus a participant table. The table lists 48 participants, 24 women and 24 men, with sex, session date and age; six archives have no row in it. The analysed sample in the preprint is the same 48: Singaporean Chinese students aged 21 to 26 (mean 22.81), right-handed and without psychological or neurological history.

## Acquisition

All scans were made on a 3 T Siemens Magnetom Prisma with a 64-channel head coil. The preprint describes a 1 mm MPRAGE and a gradient-echo EPI task run with a 2 s repetition time and 3 mm voxels. The archives hold the scanner's DICOM export. Archive CUET_0001 also contains a localizer, a field map, a resting-state BOLD run, three task runs with the scanner's motion-corrected copies and online t-maps, and diffusion trace and ADC images. The other archives were not checked for this entry.

## Annotations

There are no image labels. The study page names heart, skin conductance and thermal recordings and questionnaires as part of the protocol, but those are not in this record.

## Known limitations

The study description speaks of 50 planned participants, the archive holds 54 and the analysis used 48, without a stated reason for the difference. The preprint has not been peer reviewed and gives doi:10.21979/N9/IC672L as the data location, which is not publicly reachable. Files are raw DICOM with no BIDS conversion or event files, and the headers in CUET_0001 still carry the birth date. Use is limited to non-commercial purposes.
