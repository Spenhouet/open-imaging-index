This entry covers the cardiac MRI cohort of a retrospective single-center study of pulmonary arterial hypertension
(PAH) at a referral center in Porto Alegre, Brazil, with authors from the Pulmonary Hypertension Group of Santa Casa
de Misericórdia de Porto Alegre and UFCSPA. The authors measured right atrial (RA) area and right ventricular ejection
fraction (RVEF) on cardiac MRI and tested how each relates to established prognostic markers: right heart
catheterization (RHC) hemodynamics, echocardiographic RA area and TAPSE, six-minute walk distance and NT-proBNP. RVEF
correlated more strongly with these markers than RA area did. The images are not public. The figshare record linked to
the paper holds only its figures and tables.

## Composition

The study includes 74 patients with PAH confirmed by RHC between January and May 2018, each with cardiac MRI within
three months of the catheterization. Table 1 reports 61 women (82.4%) and a mean age of 42 years (SD 15). The causes
were idiopathic PAH (32), corrected congenital heart disease (24), connective tissue disease (14), HIV (3) and
portopulmonary hypertension (1). Forty-seven patients were in functional class I/II and 27 in class III/IV. Ten
patients died during follow-up.

## Acquisition

All scans were acquired on one 1.5 T Siemens Magnetom Aera. The protocol included a half-Fourier single-shot turbo
spin-echo (T2-weighted) sequence, ECG-gated balanced steady-state free-precession cine in four-chamber and short-axis
views, and a phase-contrast flow sequence orthogonal to the pulmonary artery trunk.

## Annotations

A chest radiologist segmented the right and left ventricles on short-axis cine images with CardiacVX (GE Healthcare)
and measured the RA area at its largest extent in the four-chamber view. Two radiologists reviewed every study, with a
third resolving disagreements.

## Known limitations

- Images, segmentations and measurements are not shared, and the paper has no data availability statement.
- Small retrospective cohort of patients already diagnosed with PAH, which the authors name as a selection bias.
- Table 1 gives the RA area without a unit.
