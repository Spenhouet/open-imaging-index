DIRECT Phase II is the second data release of the Depression Imaging REsearch ConsorTium (DIRECT), a group of Chinese research sites founded in 2017 to pool brain imaging for psychiatric research. It follows [REST-meta-MDD](../rest-meta-mdd/), which shared only region-level resting-state fMRI indices. Phase II moves to voxel- and vertex-level data processed with the surface-based DPABISurf pipeline, and it adds patients with bipolar disorder and schizophrenia so that the three disorders can be compared directly. The release lives on Science Data Bank and is described on the R-fMRI Maps Project page of the Institute of Psychology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Raw scans are not part of it.

## Composition

The release holds 3520 participants: 1660 patients with major depressive disorder and 1341 healthy controls from 23 cohorts, plus 205 patients with bipolar disorder and 314 with schizophrenia. The depression and control cohorts are described in the supplement of Chen et al. (2025), the bipolar and schizophrenia additions in Wang et al. (2026). Sex, age and per-site counts are not given on the public pages and are left out here.

## Acquisition

All resting-state fMRI and structural MRI were preprocessed with DPABISurf, and researchers at each site were trained by the consortium to harmonize processing and quality control. Shared outputs are voxel- and vertex-level BOLD time series and measures derived from structural MRI. Scanner models and imaging parameters per cohort are listed in the supplement of Chen et al. (2025), which is not openly accessible, so they are not recorded here.

## Annotations

There are no image labels. Standardized phenotypic data with the diagnostic group come with the signed agreement.

## Known limitations

Only derivatives of one fixed pipeline are available. The cohorts are retrospective and scanned with different protocols, and all sites are in China. The archives are about 2 TB and download openly, but they stay encrypted until the agreement is signed, and users must send an annual progress report.
