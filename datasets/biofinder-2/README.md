BioFINDER-2 is the second Swedish BioFINDER cohort, run by Lund University and Skåne University Hospital to find
early and reliable markers of Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease and related disorders. It began in May 2017 as a
larger, independent follow-up to BioFINDER-1, with tau PET planned for every participant alongside MRI, CSF and blood
sampling and clinical and cognitive testing. Enrolment and follow-up are still running. BioFINDER-1 is not covered by
this entry.

## Composition

The overview page gives 2,960 participants. They are spread over five cohorts: cognitively healthy adults aged 40 to 65
(mostly from the Malmö Offspring study, with an auxiliary group aged 20 to 40), cognitively healthy adults aged 66 or
older (from the Malmö Diet and Cancer study), memory clinic patients with subjective cognitive decline or mild
cognitive impairment (MCI), patients with Alzheimer's dementia, and patients with other dementias and neurodegenerative
disorders (frontotemporal dementia spectrum, vascular dementia, Parkinson's disease and related parkinsonian
disorders). The healthy cohorts were enriched to about half APOE ε4 carriers. Healthy and SCD/MCI participants are
followed for 6 to 8 years, the dementia cohorts for 2 years. Leuzy et al. (2020) analysed 613 participants enrolled up
to August 2019 with [18F]RO948 PET: 257 unimpaired controls, 154 with MCI, 100 with Alzheimer's dementia and 102 with
other disorders, including 25 with dementia with Lewy bodies and 7 with semantic variant primary progressive aphasia.

## Acquisition

All cohorts are scanned on one 3 T Siemens MAGNETOM Prisma for about an hour without contrast: 3D MPRAGE, FLAIR, SWI,
diffusion tensor imaging, functional MRI, arterial spin labelling and MR spectroscopy. Tau PET uses [18F]RO948 and
amyloid PET uses [18F]flutemetamol, the latter only in participants without dementia. Papers report both on a digital
GE Discovery MI. MRI and PET are repeated every 2 to 4 years depending on cohort. Dopamine transporter PET (first
F-DOPA, later FE-PE2I) is done in parkinsonian cases; it is not listed under tracers here because no counts are
published.

## Known limitations

- Images are not publicly released; papers state data are shared only on request with academic investigators.
- Website cohort sizes are planned targets, and the number of participants with each scan type is not published.
- Tau PET in the cohort with other dementias depended on funding.
