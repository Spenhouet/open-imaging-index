The Huntington's Disease NeuroImaging Initiative (HDNI) is the project on the LONI Image and Data Archive that holds the imaging of TRACK-HD, a multinational observational study of Huntington's disease (HD) sponsored by the CHDI Foundation. TRACK-HD followed carriers of the HD gene expansion, from about 16 years before predicted diagnosis to early stage 2 disease, alongside age- and sex-matched controls. It was run with trial-like quality control and blinded analysis to find imaging, motor, cognitive and psychiatric measures sensitive enough to serve as endpoints in therapeutic trials. The baseline paper states that the image data were archived at LONI. The LONI application page for HDNI is not open to new applicants. CHDI now distributes curated TRACK-HD and TrackOn-HD imaging in BIDS format after a signed data use agreement.

## Composition

Enrollment began in January 2008 and the baseline visits ended in August 2008. Of 381 people enrolled, 366 formed the baseline cohort: 123 controls, 120 premanifest gene carriers (split at the median predicted time to diagnosis into 62 further from and 58 nearer to onset) and 123 people with early HD (77 in stage 1, 46 in stage 2). About 55% of each group were women, and ages ranged from 18.6 to 65.7 years. Participants came from four sites: Leiden, London, Paris and Vancouver, with 90 to 96 people each. The study planned three annual follow-up visits.

## Acquisition

All participants had 3T MRI with T1- and T2-weighted protocols that were standardised across sites after a preparatory phase. The T1-weighted scan was a 3D MPRAGE acquired on Siemens or Philips scanners, with a different scanner model at each site. IXICO carried out quality control on all images.

## Annotations

The archive holds images, not labels. Group membership (control, premanifest, early HD stage), CAG repeat length and the disease-burden score are part of the clinical data. Published analyses report caudate, putamen, whole-brain and cortical thickness measures from several pipelines.

## Known limitations

No data descriptor reports the content or size of the LONI release, so only the published baseline cohort is counted here. Site and scanner model are confounded. The license text of the CHDI data use agreement is not public.
