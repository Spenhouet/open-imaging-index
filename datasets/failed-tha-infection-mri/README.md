A retrospective cohort from the IRCCS Galeazzi Orthopaedic Institute in Milan of patients whose total hip
replacement had failed and who were imaged with pelvis MRI before revision surgery. The study tested whether MRI
signs around the implant, and the number and size of regional lymph nodes on the operated side compared with the
other side, separate infected implants from aseptic failure such as loosening or polyethylene wear. The authors
deposited a spreadsheet of their per-patient readings on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0. The MR images themselves are not
offered.

## Composition

119 patients, 66 of them women, with a mean age of 66.9 years (SD 12.4). In the public spreadsheet, 38 patients are
marked as having an infected implant and 81 as aseptic failures, with the failure cause recorded for the aseptic
group.

## Acquisition

All patients had pelvis MRI at 1.5 T with a metal artifact reduction (MARS) protocol that included short tau
inversion recovery and turbo spin-echo T1- and T2-weighted sequences. Scanner vendor and further parameters are given
only in the full article, which was not openly accessible for this entry.

## Annotations

Readers scored each scan for periprosthetic bone destruction, soft-tissue mass, effusion, synovitis, lamellated
synovitis, extracapsular oedema, fibrous periprosthetic membrane, bone oedema and extracapsular collection or sinus
tract. They counted and measured inguinal, obturator and iliac lymph nodes on both sides, from which the authors
derived ratio and difference indices of node size and number. The spreadsheet also holds C-reactive protein,
erythrocyte sedimentation rate, white blood cell count and the isolated microorganism.

## Known limitations

- Only tabular readings and clinical values are public; no images or segmentations are shared.
- Single centre, single field strength and a retrospective design.
- Some spreadsheet column headers and free-text entries are in Italian.
- Details beyond the abstract (vendor, reader count, reference standard) come from the full article and are not
  recorded here.
