This record holds multimodal brain MRI of healthy medical students in China. It was deposited on Science Data Bank in January 2024 by Peng Gao and Xi-Nian Zuo of the State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Neuroscience and Learning at Beijing Normal University, and it is curated by the Chinese Color Nest Data Community. No paper or data descriptor is linked to the record, so the facts here come from the short record description and its access conditions.

## Composition

The record describes 98 healthy Chinese medical students. Volunteers were found through advertisements in colleges and communities. People were not enrolled if they smoked regularly, were pregnant, had impaired color discrimination, had a history of alcohol or drug abuse, took any current medication, had a contraindication to MRI, or had a neuropsychiatric or serious somatic disorder, including use of sedative-hypnotic drugs in the previous month. Age range, sex split and the number of sessions per person are not given.

## Acquisition

All scans were made on a 3 T scanner that the record names as Prisma. The protocol includes a high-resolution T1-weighted structural scan, resting-state functional MRI and diffusion tensor imaging. Sequence parameters, file format and the number of subjects with each scan type are not stated.

## Known limitations

Public information is limited to a few sentences, so the cohort and the protocol cannot be checked before access is granted. The public version 1 lists a single file of 46 bytes, which suggests that the imaging data are delivered separately after approval. Access requires a data use agreement that the Beijing Normal University laboratory reviews and approves. The record calls this agreement the application file Data Use Agreement; this entry links the Chinese Color Nest Data Community agreement, which the community uses for its restricted records, and its exact terms for this record should be confirmed with the depositors.
