SpineWeb Dataset 1 is a small collection of paired lumbar spine CT and MR scans offered through SpineWeb, an online
platform for spinal imaging research that also hosts several spine challenge datasets. The collection was listed as
cross-modality spinal images for the Spine Workshop. Because each CT has an MR counterpart from the same
person, the pairs can serve work that relates the two modalities, but the page gives no intended task.

## Composition

The dataset page lists 30 pairs of spinal CT and MR images of the lumbar region, with both scans of a pair taken
from the same subject. The earliest archived request page, from November 2013, listed 10 pairs. The SpineWeb news
feed reports that 10 more sets were added on 17 April 2014, bringing the total to 30. No sex, age, diagnosis or
split breakdown is published.

## Acquisition

The page does not describe the sites, scanners, MR sequences, resolution or file format of the scans.

## Annotations

None. The page states that ground truth is not yet available, and no later update adds labels.

## Known limitations

- The only documentation is a three-sentence entry on the SpineWeb datasets page. There is no paper or data
  descriptor, so the content of the release cannot be checked before requesting it.
- No license or terms of use are published for this dataset. Access starts with a request form that asks for the
  applicant's institution, a short research description and the principal investigator's contact details.
- On 2026-10-09 the SpineWeb host name did not resolve, so the facts here come from archived copies of the official
  pages. Whether the data can still be requested is unknown.
