OASIS-4 is the fourth release of the Open Access Series of Imaging Studies from Washington University in St. Louis. It
is a clinical cohort of people who were assessed for memory problems and dementia, and it stands on its own: it is not
a newer version of OASIS-3. Each participant comes with clinical, cerebrospinal fluid and neuropsychological data next
to the brain MRI.

## Composition

The project page lists 663 subjects between 21 and 94 years old and 676 MR sessions, only 13 more sessions than subjects.
Demographic, clinical, CSF and psychometric spreadsheets are provided as separate data files within the project.
The reference publication drew its Clinical Cohort from patients of the Washington University Memory Diagnostic
Center, an outpatient memory clinic, and described that cohort as soon to be released as OASIS-4. Its diagnoses
included Alzheimer disease and several non-Alzheimer causes of cognitive complaints.

## Acquisition

In the reference publication, the memory clinic patients had their MRI as part of the diagnostic evaluation for
dementia. The data are hosted on the NITRC Image
Repository and can be downloaded with the official OASIS scripts, which convert the scans with dcm2niix to NIfTI in a
BIDS layout (BIDS version 1.0.1) with JSON sidecars. Scanner models, field strengths and the list of sequences are not
published for this release.

## Annotations

No manual labels. MR sessions come with FreeSurfer post-processing outputs.

## Known limitations

- No data descriptor exists. The reference publication analyses a 579-patient precursor of the cohort, not the
  released set, so its numbers are not repeated here.
- Sex, diagnosis and scanner breakdowns of the released cohort are not published.
