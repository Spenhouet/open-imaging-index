MOSAIC (Multi-Omics Spatial Atlas in Cancer) is a multi-center cancer study led by Owkin with academic hospitals in
Switzerland, Germany, France and the US. It profiles tumor samples with spatial transcriptomics and single-cell RNA
sequencing alongside digitized H&E histology, bulk RNA sequencing, whole exome sequencing and about 100 clinical
variables per patient. The goal is a large reference for finding cancer subtypes, drug targets, treatment response
markers and biomarkers with machine learning. A 60-patient subset is shared with academic researchers as a separate
entry ([MOSAIC Window](../mosaic-window/)).

## Composition

The project website lists 2,725 patients in 11 cancer types: diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, non-small cell lung
cancer, ovarian cancer, glioblastoma, bladder cancer, breast cancer, pancreatic cancer, head and neck squamous cell
carcinoma, mesothelioma, colorectal cancer and gastric cancer. The per-type counts add up to 2,248, fewer than the
total, and the site does not explain the gap. It also gives a count for each data type without saying whether it
means patients or samples: 2,666 for H&E whole-slide images, 2,371 for spatial transcriptomics, 2,266 for whole
exome sequencing, 2,168 for single-cell RNA sequencing and 2,114 for bulk RNA sequencing. The preprint, written
earlier, reports more than 1,900 patients as of April 2025.

## Acquisition

The founding partners are Lausanne University Hospital, Charité in Berlin, University Hospital Erlangen, Gustave
Roussy near Paris and the University of Pittsburgh. Tumor samples are formalin-fixed and paraffin-embedded. According
to the preprint, all participating centers scan the H&E slides on a Leica Aperio CS2 scanner at 40x magnification.

## Annotations

No image annotations such as tumor outlines are described. Each patient comes with clinical variables.

## Known limitations

The full cohort is not openly available. Owkin offers it in the paid tier of its K Pro platform and publishes no terms
for it, so it is unclear what users may download or do with the data. The counts on the website change as the study
grows and carry no date.
