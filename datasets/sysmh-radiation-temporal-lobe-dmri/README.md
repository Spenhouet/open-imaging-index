This Science Data Bank record comes from a retrospective study at the Department of Radiology of Sun Yat-sen Memorial
Hospital, Sun Yat-sen University, in Guangzhou, China. The study asked which diffusion MRI model best separates a
temporal lobe damaged by radiotherapy from the healthy temporal lobe on the other side. It compared the apparent
diffusion coefficient with parameters from diffusion tensor imaging (DTI), diffusion kurtosis imaging (DKI), mean
apparent propagator MRI (MAP-MRI) and neurite orientation dispersion and density imaging (NODDI), using ROC analysis.
The record was published on 30 January 2026 as version 1 under CC0.

## Composition

The description reports 80 patients who developed temporal lobe necrosis on one side after radiotherapy for
nasopharyngeal carcinoma. Each patient serves as their own control, with the unaffected temporal lobe as the
reference. Age, sex, time since radiotherapy and scanner are not given. The only file is a small results table with
one row per temporal lobe and columns for DKI radial kurtosis, DTI radial diffusivity, MAP-MRI non-Gaussianity
anisotropy, NODDI intracellular volume fraction and ADC, plus label columns.

## Acquisition

All patients had conventional MRI and a diffusion MRI examination. All five models were fitted to the same diffusion
data. Field strength, vendor, b-values, number of directions and the conventional sequences are not described.

## Known limitations

- No images are public, only one table of derived values, so the cohort cannot be reanalysed from the scans.
- No study paper is linked from the record and none was found, so the design is known only from the record's
  description and the table's column names.
- Demographics and acquisition parameters are missing.
- Single centre, retrospective, and limited to patients with unilateral necrosis.
