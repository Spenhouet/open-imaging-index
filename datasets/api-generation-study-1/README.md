Generation Study 1 was a phase 2/3 prevention trial of the Alzheimer's Prevention Initiative, run by Banner
Alzheimer's Institute with Novartis and Amgen. It enrolled cognitively unimpaired people aged 60 to 75 who carry two
copies of APOE4, with or without raised brain amyloid. Cohort I received the active amyloid vaccine CAD106 or
placebo, and Cohort II the BACE inhibitor umibecestat or placebo. Both cohorts stopped early in 2019 after people on
umibecestat showed mild cognitive worsening that later reversed. Anonymized clinical data and images are requested
through the LONI Image and Data Archive.

## Composition

480 participants were randomized: 65 in Cohort I (42 on CAD106, 23 on placebo) and 415 in Cohort II. Cohort I came
from 28 sites in five countries, and Cohort II from 10 countries. In Cohort I, 44 of the 65 were women, nearly all
were White, and about two thirds were amyloid positive at baseline. The provider page says screening and longitudinal
data are shared, so volunteers who were screened but not randomized are also part of the archive.

## Acquisition

Screening included brain MRI, amyloid PET or a lumbar puncture, and tau PET. The study plan lists volumetric and
resting-state functional MRI, amyloid PET, FDG PET (this study only) and tau PET. Safety MRI for ARIA was done every
six months in the first year and yearly after that. In Cohort I, volumetric T1-weighted MRI was acquired on 3T systems
at screening and at weeks 26, 52 and 104. Most amyloid PET in Cohort I used florbetapir, and a few participants had
one of two other tracers.

## Annotations

No image annotations are described. Central readers confirmed ARIA on safety MRI: three cases on CAD106 (one ARIA-E,
two ARIA-H). Central MRI reads, volumetric measures and amyloid PET values such as SUVR come with
the clinical data.

## Known limitations

- The trial ended early, so treatment and follow-up fall far short of the planned 5 to 8 years. In Cohort I,
  treatment lasted 6 to 48 months.
- MRI sequences other than the volumetric T1-weighted scan, and the tau PET tracer, are not named in the open papers.
- The Data Transfer Agreement that approved requesters sign is not published.
- Sex, age and ARIA counts for Cohort II are reported only pooled with Generation Study 2.
