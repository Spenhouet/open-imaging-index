The Cardiac Atlas Project (CAP) hosts cardiac MRI from DETERMINE, a prospective multicentre randomised trial in
patients with coronary artery disease and mild to moderate left ventricular dysfunction. The trial tested whether an
implantable cardioverter defibrillator added to medical therapy improves survival in patients whose infarct covers a
large share of the left ventricular mass. CAP collects such exams to build statistical atlases of heart shape and
motion, and the DETERMINE collection has been used for atlas studies of remodelling after myocardial infarction.

## Composition

The CAP page lists 450 DETERMINE cases, each with MR images, and some with 3D left ventricular models fitted
semi-automatically with the Cardiac Image Modeller software from the University of Auckland. The 2011 CAP paper
reported 470 contributed datasets from patients with myocardial infarction at that time. CAP also holds limited
clinical variables for the contributed cases, such as age, sex, height, weight, blood pressure, and classifications
for hypertension, diabetes, smoking and NYHA class.

## Acquisition

Exams were acquired at the trial field centres on 1.5 T or 3 T scanners from Siemens, GE and Philips. A typical exam
has retrospectively gated SSFP cine images in breath-holds of 8 to 15 seconds, with short-axis slices covering the
whole heart and long-axis slices in four-chamber, two-chamber and outflow tract views. Late gadolinium enhancement
images, an inversion recovery segmented gradient echo sequence 10 to 20 minutes after contrast injection, are taken
at the same positions as the cine slices. Images are stored as DICOM objects.

## Annotations

The DETERMINE MRI core laboratory at Northwestern University analysed the images with QMASS and contributed the
ventricular contours to CAP. The left ventricular models were derived from the images with semi-automatic fitting.

## Known limitations

- Download is suspended while the data sharing agreement with the contributor is renewed.
- Only participants who gave separate consent to share with CAP are included.
- Access needs an approved research proposal and a signed agreement per project, and commercial use needs the
  permission of the trial holder and sponsor.
- The CAP page gives the inclusion threshold as an infarct of at least 15% of left ventricular mass, the 2011 paper
  as at least 10%.
- No breakdown by sex, age, site or scanner is published for the hosted cases.
