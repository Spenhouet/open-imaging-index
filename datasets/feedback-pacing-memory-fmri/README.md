This Science Data Bank record comes from a task fMRI study at Guizhou Normal University on how people allocate study
time. Participants learned material either at their own pace or at a pace set by the experimenter, and the study
varied whether feedback was given at all and whether it signalled reward or punishment. The record was deposited by
Liangzhi Jia, first published on 3 April 2026 and updated to version 2 on 7 April 2026. It is curated by the
Psychological Science Data Bank community.

## Composition

The record description reports 60 participants with behavioral and fMRI results. Age, sex, health status and
recruitment are not stated. The deposit has 173 files (about 107 MB) in two folders. The fMRI folder has one SPM model
per task phase (learning, judgment of learning and test) and per comparison (feedback against no feedback, reward
against punishment), six models in all. Each model has beta, contrast, t and F maps, residual and mask images, and
the SPM.mat and batch files. Some folders add region-of-interest definitions and extracted values for regions such
as the insula, angular gyrus, superior frontal gyrus and caudate, and rendered figures. The behavioral folder holds
test-phase accuracy tables and SPSS output files.

## Acquisition

The record names task fMRI only. Scanner, field strength, sequences, structural scans and preprocessing are not
described.

## Known limitations

- Only group-level statistics are public. Raw, preprocessed and per-subject images are not shared, so the cohort
  cannot be reanalysed at the subject level from this record.
- No study paper or data descriptor is linked from the record and none was found, so the design is known only from
  the title, keywords, short description and folder names.
- Demographics and acquisition details are missing.
- File and folder names are in Chinese.
