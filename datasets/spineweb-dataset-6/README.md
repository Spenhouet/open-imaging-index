SpineWeb Dataset 6 is a small MRI collection for finding and segmenting the intervertebral discs of the lower spine.
It was contributed to SpineWeb, a collection of spine imaging datasets, by Guoyan Zheng of the Institute for Surgical
Technology and Biomechanics at the University of Bern. The only description is the short SpineWeb entry; no paper or
data descriptor is linked from it.

## Composition

8 anonymized patients. Each patient contributes four co-registered 3D volumes from a single Dixon acquisition: an
in-phase, an opposed-phase, a fat and a water image. Every patient has at least 7 intervertebral discs in the field
of view, covering the levels from T11 to L5. Images and masks are stored as NIfTI files, four image volumes and one
mask volume per patient. The page gives no age, sex or diagnosis information.

## Acquisition

All patients were scanned on a 1.5 T Siemens MRI scanner with a Dixon protocol, which reconstructs the four aligned
high-resolution volumes from one acquisition. Sequence parameters, voxel size and scan sites are not stated.

## Annotations

Each disc has a manual reference segmentation, delivered as one binary mask volume per patient. The page does not
say who drew the masks or how they were checked.

## Known limitations

- Very small: 8 patients from one scanner type and one protocol.
- No demographics, clinical data, acquisition parameters or annotation protocol are published.
- Access is by contacting the contributor; no license or data use terms are published for this dataset.
- The SpineWeb host (spineweb.digitalimaginggroup.ca) did not resolve when this entry was checked on 2026-10-09; the
  description was read from an Internet Archive copy of the page from June 2024.
