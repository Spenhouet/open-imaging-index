This entry covers the functional MRI cohort of a 2021 NeuroImage study by Steines, Nagels, Kircher and Straube at the
Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of Philipps-Universität Marburg. The study asked how the left and right
inferior frontal gyrus (IFG) respond to different kinds of co-speech gestures. It found that abstract speech with
metaphoric gestures and concrete speech with unrelated gestures both raised bilateral IFG activation compared with
concrete speech paired with fitting iconic gestures, with a leftward bias for the metaphoric condition and a
rightward bias for the unrelated one. The raw scans are not released. The authors posted derived maps, masks,
scripts and behavioral tables on OSF.

## Composition

74 subjects took part. The paper abstract does not give sex, age or clinical status, so none of these are listed
here. The OSF deposit has one first-level folder per subject, numbered sub-01 to sub-74.

## Acquisition

During scanning, subjects watched 5 second video clips and judged whether the content of the speech and the gesture
matched. The design has conditions for abstract speech with related metaphoric gestures, concrete speech with related
iconic gestures and concrete speech with unrelated gestures; the OSF data also include an abstract speech with
unrelated gesture condition that the paper does not analyse. Preprocessing in SPM12 covered realignment,
normalization and smoothing. Scanner, field strength and sequence parameters could not be confirmed from an openly
reachable source and are left out.

## Annotations

The OSF project provides, per subject, SPM contrast and t maps for each of the four conditions, plus group-level
flexible factorial results, eigenvariates from three IFG volumes of interest, left, right and bilateral IFG masks
built from AAL regions, hit and false alarm rates, and item scores on the BAG gesture scale.

## Known limitations

- Only derived statistical maps are public; raw and preprocessed functional images are not shared.
- No license or data use terms are stated for the OSF files.
- Demographics and scanner details are missing from this entry because the full paper text could not be opened.
