MEMENTO is a French clinic-based cohort set up under the 2008-2012 national Alzheimer plan to follow people who
consult a memory clinic before they develop dementia. It is run from the clinical epidemiology unit (CIC 1401-EC) in
Bordeaux, sponsored by Bordeaux University Hospital and registered as NCT01926249. Imaging was harmonised across sites
by CATI. The SHIVA-CMB microbleed detector (Tsuchida et al. 2024) was evaluated on its baseline T2*-weighted scans.

## Composition

2,323 non-demented adults were enrolled at 26 university-based memory clinics between April 2011 and June 2014. At
baseline 61.8% were women and the mean age was 70.9 years (SD 8.7). The design paper sorts 2,313 of them into five
cognitive groups: 370 with an isolated cognitive complaint and 1,943 with single- or multi-domain, amnestic or
non-amnestic mild cognitive impairment. Participants were seen every six months for five years, with clinical,
neuropsychological, lifestyle and biobank data; the DPUK record lists 1,481 participants at the fifth annual visit.

## Acquisition

The harmonised MRI protocol is compatible with ADNI and contains a 3D T1-weighted scan, 2D FLAIR, a 2D T2*-weighted
gradient echo, a 2D T2-weighted turbo spin echo and, as options, resting-state BOLD and diffusion. 86% of participants
were scanned at 3 T and the rest at 1.5 T, on systems from several vendors. Structural MRI was planned at baseline,
month 24 and month 48. FDG PET was optional and done in 60% of participants at baseline; the DPUK record also lists
amyloid PET. In SHIVA-CMB, the T2*-GRE used TR 650 ms, TE 20 ms, flip angle 20 degrees and 1.0 x 1.0 x 2.5 mm voxels.

## Annotations

No voxel labels are described. For 1,992 participants, a trained rater counted cerebral microbleeds on the baseline
T2*-GRE with the Microbleed Anatomical Rating Scale, checked by a neuroradiologist; SHIVA-CMB reports 1,503
microbleeds in total. Hippocampal volume, white matter lesion volume, cortical thickness and regional FDG uptake were
derived centrally.

## Known limitations

- Not open. Each project needs an approved proposal, and the charter requires specific authorisation for raw images.
- Analysis through DPUK happens on a remote portal; whether raw images can be released outside it is not stated.
- Sex, field strength and PET counts are published as percentages only, so they are not in the stats table.
- The number of participants with a T2*-GRE is known only for the 1,992 used in SHIVA-CMB.
