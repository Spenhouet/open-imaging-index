FOCAS is a prospective follow-up study of sporadic cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA) at Leiden University Medical
Center, started in February 2018. It shares its visit protocol with AURORA, the centre's study of Dutch-type hereditary
CAA, and the two are often analysed together to study how CAA markers on MRI progress over time. The data are not
public; papers from the cohort offer them on request from the corresponding author.

## Composition

The LUMC study page reports 126 participants seen for one or more research visits; visits have ended. The study
registration (NL-OMON52554) asks for age 50 or older and probable CAA by the Boston criteria v2.0 without a family
history of hereditary CAA, and also lists age- and sex-matched healthy controls. Published subsets: 67 sporadic CAA
participants screened at baseline, of whom 55 (mean age 70) had a 3 T MRI after one year; and 5 patients with one-year
follow-up scans in a siderosis quantification study.

## Acquisition

Participants were seen yearly for 3 T MRI on a Philips Achieva with a 32-channel head coil, 7 T MRI, blood and CSF
sampling and clinical questionnaires. The 3 T protocol includes 4-echo SWI (TR 31 ms, first TE 7.2 ms, 0.6 x 0.6 x 1 mm,
130 slices) and T1- and T2-weighted imaging; a visually stimulated BOLD fMRI measures vascular reactivity.

## Annotations

Trained raters scored lobar microbleeds and macrobleeds, cortical superficial siderosis (cSS), convexity subarachnoid
haemorrhage, white matter hyperintensities and centrum semiovale perivascular spaces at baseline and follow-up, and
measured WMH volume. A cSS quantification study built semi-automatic voxel masks of cSS on SWI (vesselness filter,
seeded region growing, manual removal of false positives) for 5 FOCAS patients at baseline and one year, together with
15 patients from AURORA. Repeat masks by the same rater overlapped with a mean Dice of 0.75.

## Known limitations

- No public access route, license or data use terms.
- Single centre and single 3 T scanner, so models trained on it may not transfer to other protocols.
- Published counts describe subsets; the sex and age make-up of all 126 participants is not published.
- The cSS masks cover only 20 patients in total, and the tool code is available only on request.
